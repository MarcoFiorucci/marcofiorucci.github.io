## Abstract

My primary research interests lie at the intersection of representation learning and multimodal alignment across vision, language, and 3D data. **RankAlign** proposes a computationally efficient approach based on rank transformations to align cross-modal representations without paired data.

## Key Contributions

- **Efficient Alignment**: Leverages rank transformations for fast cross-modal correspondences.
- **Unsupervised / Unpaired**: Recovers alignment without needing matching pair labels.
- **Generalization**: Demonstrates strong performance across computer vision and multimodal tasks.
